2. **Data retention wording**

   - Flag: Review whether specific retention periods or categories should be defined

3. **Limitation of liability**
   - Current: Standard disclaimer of indirect damages
   - Flag: Review enforceability under applicable Indian law

4. **Privacy rights language**
