## Cross-cutting language decisions

### Evidence and proof

Keep evidence, demonstration, and “something to show” only when they describe the broader employability philosophy, a hiring conversation, or a clearly illustrative journey. Remove or defer language that says Pathwisse currently creates, stores, scores, uploads, or organizes evidence.

### Direction

Keep direction as a strategic starting point. Do not claim role recommendations, assessment, matching, role discovery UI, or target-role functionality.

### Progress and readiness

Do not claim progress tracking, readiness calculation, skills measurement, dashboards, or earlier institutional visibility. Use “what comes next” and “connected journey” as conceptual language.

### Programs

Keep programs as a conceptual intervention in the journey. Do not publish catalogue items, delivery formats, duration, pricing, or outcomes until verified.

## Publication boundary

The refined homepage communicates the education-to-employability problem, Pathwisse’s beliefs, intended approach, audiences, and next conversations. It does not establish that any capability-specific workflow exists. Re-audit this document when authoritative product access becomes available.
